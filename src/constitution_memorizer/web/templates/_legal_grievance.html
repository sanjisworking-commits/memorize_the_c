2. What You Can Report

You may use this mechanism to raise a grievance concerning matters including:

  • your Recall the C account;
  • access to or deletion of your account;
  • your personal data;
  • correction or updating of personal data;
  • withdrawal of consent;
  • Google Sign-In;
  • Google Calendar connection or disconnection;
  • suspected unauthorised access;
  • security concerns;
  • subscription or payment issues;
  • technical problems;
  • inaccurate educational material;
  • intellectual-property concerns;
  • misuse of the Service;
  • an issue report that has not been resolved; or
  • another matter relating to Recall the C.

3. How to Submit a Grievance

You may submit a grievance by emailing {{ email(op.grievance_email) }}. Signed-in users may also use the in-app Contact us control.

Please use the subject line:

Grievance — Recall the C

To help us investigate your grievance efficiently, please provide:

  1. your name;
  2. the email address or mobile number associated with your Recall the C account, where applicable;
  3. a clear description of the issue;
  4. the relevant page, feature, transaction or event;
  5. screenshots or supporting information, if useful;
  6. the action you would like us to consider; and
  7. any previous Recall the C support or issue-report reference number.

Please do not send passwords, OTPs, complete payment-card details, Google OAuth tokens or other credentials.

4. Acknowledgment and Resolution

Recall the C aims to acknowledge a valid grievance within 24 hours of receipt.

We aim to investigate and resolve ordinary grievances within seven days, where reasonably possible.

Some matters may require additional time because of their complexity, identity-verification requirements, involvement of an external service provider, legal obligations or investigation of a security incident.

Where a different timeframe is prescribed by applicable law, the legally applicable timeframe will apply.

If additional time is required, we will endeavour to keep you informed of the status of the grievance.

5. Privacy and Personal Data Requests

If your grievance concerns personal data, you may request, subject to applicable law:

  • information about personal data processed about you;
  • access to relevant personal data;
  • correction of inaccurate information;
  • completion or updating of information;
  • deletion or erasure of personal data;
  • withdrawal of consent; or
  • information about relevant processing activities.

We may ask you to provide sufficient information to verify your identity before acting on a request.

6. Google Account and Calendar Complaints

For grievances concerning Google integration, please specify whether the issue relates to:

  • Google Sign-In;
  • connecting Google Calendar;
  • a Recall the C-created Calendar;
  • revision events;
  • revoking Google access; or
  • deletion of stored Google authorisation information.

Recall the C will investigate matters concerning data within its control.

You may independently revoke Recall the C’s access through your Google Account settings.

Calendar events or calendars already stored in your Google Account may also be managed directly through Google Calendar.

7. Security Incidents

If you believe that your account or personal data has been compromised, please state:

URGENT — SECURITY

in the subject line of your communication.

Do not send passwords, OTPs, access tokens, private authentication credentials or full payment information.

We may prioritise such reports according to the apparent security risk.

8. Content Accuracy and Corrections

Recall the C welcomes reports concerning possible errors in constitutional text, amendment information, explanations, visual material or other educational content.

Content complaints will be reviewed against appropriate source material.

Recall the C is an educational platform and does not adjudicate legal disputes or provide authoritative legal interpretations.

9. Payment and Subscription Grievances

For payment-related matters, please provide the transaction reference number, date and account identifier where available.

Do not provide your full card number, CVV, UPI PIN, banking password or OTP.

Where payment processing is performed by a third-party payment provider, we may need to coordinate with that provider to investigate the transaction.

10. Escalation

If you believe that your grievance has not been appropriately addressed, you may request an internal escalation by replying to the grievance response and stating:

Request for Escalation

Where applicable law provides a right to approach a regulatory authority, statutory board, Grievance Appellate Committee, court or other authority after completion of the internal grievance process, nothing in this policy restricts that right.

11. Abuse of the Grievance Mechanism

Please submit genuine grievances and provide accurate information.

Recall the C may take appropriate measures against automated spam, impersonation, deliberately false claims, harassment or misuse of grievance and reporting mechanisms.

This does not prevent any person from raising a genuine concern in good faith.

12. Confidentiality

Information submitted through the grievance process will be used for investigating, resolving, documenting and, where necessary, complying with legal obligations relating to the grievance.

Personal information submitted during this process will be handled in accordance with the Recall the C Privacy Policy.
