3. Google Sign-In

If you choose to sign in using Google, Google may provide Recall the C with information that you have authorised Google to share with us, such as your Google account identifier, name and email address.

We use this information to:

Recall the C does not receive your Google password.

4. Google Calendar Data

Google Calendar integration is optional.

If you choose to connect it, Recall the C may request the Google Calendar permission:

{{ google_calendar_scope }}

This permission enables Recall the C to create a secondary Google Calendar and view, create, modify and delete events on calendars created by Recall the C.

What Recall the C accesses

When you enable this feature, Recall the C may process:

  • the identifier of a Google Calendar created by Recall the C;
  • identifiers of revision events created by Recall the C;
  • event dates and information required to create your revision schedule;
  • synchronisation status;
  • information indicating whether your Google Calendar account is connected; and
  • OAuth authorisation credentials necessary to maintain the authorised connection.

Why we use Google Calendar data

Google Calendar access is used solely to provide user-facing revision scheduling and calendar synchronisation functionality requested by you.

For example, Recall the C may create a secondary calendar named for the Recall the C revision schedule and add revision events corresponding to dates calculated by your learning schedule.

What Recall the C does not do

  • Recall the C does not use this Calendar permission to access your unrelated personal calendars.
  • Recall the C does not use Google Calendar information for advertising.
  • Recall the C does not sell Google user data.
  • Recall the C does not use Google Calendar data to build advertising profiles.
  • Recall the C does not use Google Calendar data to train general-purpose artificial-intelligence models.

Recall the C’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, where applicable.

5. OAuth Tokens and Google Authorisation Credentials

Such credentials are used only for the authorised Google integration and must be protected using appropriate technical and organisational safeguards.

They are not sold or used for advertising.

8. Disconnecting Google Calendar

You may disconnect Recall the C from Google Calendar using the controls provided by Recall the C and/or revoke Recall the C’s access through your Google Account.

After disconnection:

  • Recall the C will stop making new Calendar API requests on your behalf;
  • stored Google authorisation credentials associated with the connection will be deleted or invalidated in accordance with our retention and security procedures; and
  • revision events or secondary calendars already created within your Google Calendar may remain in your Google account unless they are separately deleted.

You may delete such calendars or events using Google Calendar.
