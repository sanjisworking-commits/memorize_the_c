The gap

Recognition feels like knowledge. Recall proves it.

“I recognise this.”

Familiar on the page — and gone the moment you look away.

“I can reproduce this.”

Retrieved from memory, in your own words, without the text in front of you.

The method

Four steps that turn the C into lasting memory.

  1. 01

    Capture the C

    Pin down the exact unit worth learning — a clause, a definition, a precise line.

  2. 02

    Clarify the C

    Break dense wording into pieces small enough to hold in your head at once.

  3. 03

    Recall the C

    Reproduce it without looking — typing, cloze blanks, first letters, or aloud.

  4. 04

    Continue the C

    Return right before it fades — each review stretches how long it stays.
