The gap

Recognition feels like knowledge. Recall proves it.

Take one line of Article 21. On the page it feels effortless. Look away, and the gap appears.

1 On the page

No person shall be deprived of his life or personal liberty except according to procedure established by law.

Every word is right there. Of course you know it.

2 From memory

No person shall be   of his   or personal   except according to   established by  .

Same line, five words gone. This is the only test that counts.

Recall the C lives in that second panel — closing the distance between reading a clause and reproducing it.

The method

Four steps that turn the C into lasting memory.

  1. 01

    Capture the C

    Pin down the exact unit worth learning — a clause, a definition, a precise line.

  2. 02

    Clarify the C

    Break dense wording into pieces small enough to hold in your head at once.

  3. 03

    Recall the C

    Reproduce it without looking — typing, cloze blanks, first letters, or aloud.

  4. 04

    Continue the C

    A review calendar brings each unit back on a widening schedule — day 1, 3, 7, 14, 30 — right before it fades.

Your review calendar spaces each unit out as it sticks.

Every successful recall pushes the next review further away — and marks the unit closer to mastered.
